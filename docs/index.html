The method

Season, element, sign.

Three threads run through every reading. Where the year stands, which element is loudest in your life, and the living thing that keeps crossing your path.

The season

Every question lands somewhere on the turning year. What is ready to be cut back, what is ready to be planted, and what needs one more winter. I start where the year actually is, not where you wish it were.

The element

Earth, water, fire, or air. One of them is speaking over the others in your life right now. Naming it tells us what the situation needs, and what it will not accept.

The sign

The animal you keep seeing. The plant that grows where it should not. The land repeats itself until it is heard. I listen for the repetition and put it into plain words.

Rowan, pressed the autumn I began. It goes out in my coat pocket to every reading.
The reader

I am Willow.

I learned to read the land before I learned much else. My grandmother read the weather in the soil and the animals before she read anything printed, and she held one rule above the rest. Finish the reading or do not start it.

I live at the edge of a Pacific Northwest woodland, among cedar and rowan and the long wet season, and I do not read at a desk. Your question comes outside with me and is read where it lives: under the cedar in the rain, at the creek with my hands in the water, on open ground with the wind moving. Earth, water, fire, and air are not symbols to me. They are where I work.

The reading is prepared alone, in ceremony. A candle when the air is still, cedar smoke, the season for company. You are never on camera and never on a call. You receive the reading in writing, to keep.

The land does not flatter. That is why I trust it, and why you can.
How it arrives

Four steps, then the letter.

The same quiet process for every reading, whichever depth you choose.

First

Choose your reading

Pick the depth that fits your question, from a single sign to the whole ground. Purchase through the shop.

Second

Tell me where you stand

A short private form asks your question and what has been repeating around you. A few honest lines are enough.

Third

I read outdoors

Your question goes out the door with me, alone, in whatever weather the day brings. I work through season, element, and sign on the living ground until the reading is finished. Never rushed, never templated.

Fourth

The reading comes to you

A written document arrives by email. No video, no live session. A keepsake you can return to when the season turns again.
